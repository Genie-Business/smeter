The service

smeter lets tenants buy prepaid electricity for the meter in their home, and lets property owners manage their meters, set their price per kWh and receive payment for the electricity their tenants buy.

Accounts

  • Property owners must give accurate details, including a payout bank account in their own name, and keep their password safe.
  • Tenants sign in with their meter number and a one-time code sent to the email address their property owner registered.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else is using it.

Buying electricity

  • Prices per kWh are set by the property owner, within the limits set by regulation. The app shows the units, any charges and the total before you pay.
  • Payments are processed by Paystack. A payment processing fee and, where it applies, the meter's monthly service charge may be added, as shown before you pay.
  • Once a payment is confirmed we issue an electricity token for the units bought. It is sent to the meter where the meter is connected, and you can also key it in on the meter.
  • Tokens that have been issued and loaded can't be refunded. If you paid and didn't receive a working token, contact us and we'll reissue it or refund you.

Property owners

  • The money for electricity your tenants buy is settled to your payout account through Paystack, less any fees and service charges shown in the app. You are responsible for paying your electricity distribution company for the power supplied to your property.
  • You must only add meters you are entitled to manage, set lawful prices, and use remote disconnection fairly and lawfully.
  • A monthly service charge applies to meters that connect over the mobile network, paid as you choose in the app.

Acceptable use

Don't misuse smeter: no tampering with meters or tokens, no fraud, no attempts to access other people's accounts or meters, and no interference with the service.

Availability

We work to keep smeter available, but it depends on mobile networks, the meter network and payment providers, so we can't promise it will always be uninterrupted. If something goes wrong with a purchase, we'll help put it right.
