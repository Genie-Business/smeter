Good to know

Is the app free?+

Yes. smeter is free to download for tenants and property owners. You only pay for the power you buy.

Do I need internet to load units?+

No. Buying needs a connection; loading a token on a manual-token meter doesn’t. Just type it into the in-home keypad.

How do I pay?+

Card, bank transfer or USSD, through a secure checkout. We never see or store your card details.

I own a building. How do I start?+

Book a free site survey. We check the wiring, install a meter in each unit and get you set up in the app.

What if something goes wrong?+

Tap Help in the app to report a payment or meter issue, or call us. Every ticket gets a reference and goes to the right team.
