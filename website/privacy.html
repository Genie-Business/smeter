Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

smeter is a prepaid electricity app for rented homes, operated by Genie Business Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the smeter app, website and admin services, why we use it, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

Property owners give us:

  • name, email address, phone number and a password (stored only as a secure hash);
  • property address, the prices they set per kWh and the meters they manage;
  • payout bank details (bank, account number and account name), which we pass to Paystack to create the owner's settlement account.

Tenants: a property owner adds the tenant's name, phone number and email address when they link a meter. Tenants sign in with the meter number and a one-time code sent to that email address.

From using smeter we keep:

  • purchases: amounts, units, tokens, dates and payment references;
  • meter data from the meter network: consumption, voltage, current, connection status and alarms, for the meters linked to your account;
  • basic technical data needed to run the service, such as sign-in sessions and error logs.

We don't collect your card or bank login details: payments are entered on Paystack's checkout, not in smeter. We don't use advertising trackers and we don't sell personal data.

How we use it

  • to provide the service: sign-in, buying power, issuing and delivering electricity tokens, usage charts and remote meter control;
  • to process payments and pay property owners their share through Paystack;
  • to send service messages such as sign-in codes, receipts and alerts;
  • to prevent fraud, keep the service secure, and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations;
  • to provide support when you contact us.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that run smeter on our behalf, and only what each needs:

  • Paystack: payment processing and payouts to property owners;
  • the meter network operator (the head-end supplied with your meter): to issue tokens and read meter data;
  • email delivery and hosting and database providers that store and send data for us.

A tenant's purchases and meter data are visible to the property owner of that meter, and an owner's name and price per kWh are shown to their tenants. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while the account is active. Payment and transaction records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is deleted. Sign-in codes expire within minutes.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see and update most of your details in the app, or ask us for a copy.
  • Deleting your account: property owners can delete their account in the app (Profile → Delete account), or see how to delete your account.
  • Other rights: you can object to or ask us to restrict processing, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, passwords are hashed, and access to our systems is restricted to staff who need it.

Children

smeter is not intended for children under 18.

Changes and contact

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date above. Questions or requests: hello@smeter.app.
