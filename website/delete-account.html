Delete your account

Last updated 21 November 2026

You can delete your smeter property-owner account at any time.

In the app

  1. Open smeter and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

Without the app

Email hello@smeter.app from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my smeter account". We'll confirm it's you and delete it within 7 days.

What happens

  • Your account is deactivated straight away: you're signed out on every device, and you can't sign in or reset the password again.
  • Payment and transaction records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, then deleted.
  • Meters you managed stop being managed from your account. If your tenants still need to buy power, contact us first so we can move the meters to whoever takes over.

Tenants

Tenants don't create an account: access comes from the meter their property owner linked. To have your details removed, ask your property owner to remove the meter, or email us.
