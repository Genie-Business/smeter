Owner questions

What does it cost?+

The survey is free. Meter pricing depends on the number of units and phase type. We’ll quote after the survey. There’s no monthly software fee.

How do I earn from it?+

You set the price per kWh. The difference between the utility cost and your price is your margin, settled to your bank with every purchase.

What if a tenant owes me money?+

Set a debt and a percentage. A share of each of their purchases goes to you until it’s cleared.

Do my meters need GPRS?+

No. GPRS meters receive units automatically and give live data; manual-token meters work anywhere because tenants key the token into the keypad.
